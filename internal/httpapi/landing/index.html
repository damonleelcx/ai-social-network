      II · Why Suki is different

      Not an algorithm with a chat box

      Most apps hand you a deck. SynAgent gives you someone to talk to, who gets a better sense of your type each time.

      She has a personality

      Suki has her own character, face and voice, and she answers as she thinks instead of making you wait. You can choose how she talks to you.

      She learns your type

      Her introductions improve with what you accept, what you pass on, and what you say after your dates.

      She keeps things respectful

      Explicit photos, messages and profile text are filtered, and you can block or report anyone.

      Not only dates

      Nobody to see a movie with tonight? Just tell Suki, and it works for a meal, a trip or a business contact too. She asks people in your city, and you pick who to go with.

      She finds the place

      She shortlists public places you'd both enjoy. You each pick a favorite privately, and the date is set once you both confirm.

      III · Your control

      You hold the save file

      Suki is an AI, not a person. What she remembers belongs to you.

      • Read what Suki remembers about you.
      • Delete any of it, or reset what she has learned.
      • Export your data whenever you like.
