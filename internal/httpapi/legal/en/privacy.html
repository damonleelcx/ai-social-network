Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Heros Agents ("we", "us") collects through the SynAgent app and this website, how we use it, who receives it, and the choices you have. Questions and requests go to support@heros-agent.space.

Summary

  • We don't sell your personal data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers in the app.
  • Your messages, profile and photos are processed by an AI model provider in China so that Suki can work and so that content can be moderated.
  • Other people's feedback after dates can affect who you are introduced to. These internal evaluations aren't shown in the app.
  • You can delete your account and export much of your data in Settings.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: your account, Suki's conversations, introductions, messaging, date planning, voice playback and notifications.
  • To personalize it: learning what kind of person suits you and improving future introductions.
  • To keep people safe: checking content, handling reports, and preventing abuse, fraud and repeat offenses.
  • To run the Service: sending account emails, fixing problems, securing our systems and keeping backups.
  • To meet legal duties: responding to lawful requests and protecting our rights and those of others.

We don't use your data to train our own AI models. If you live in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a safe service, legal obligations, and your consent for optional sensitive details you choose to give us. You can withdraw that consent by removing the details.

How matching and automated decisions work

Suki introduces people automatically in the same city. Your preferences and dealbreakers filter who can be considered. The people who remain are ranked using your profile, what you've accepted and declined, what you've told her after dates, and an AI model's assessment of how well you might suit each other.

Post-date feedback from others can influence who you're introduced to. Its effect is limited, and it needs feedback from several people. These internal evaluations aren't displayed in the app. We don't use race or ethnicity, we don't score photos for looks, and we use religion and politics only as a dealbreaker you set yourself. Age and height can be used in matching.

You can ask how an automated decision about you was made, or ask for a person to review it, by writing to support@heros-agent.space.

Content moderation

Explicit content isn't allowed. Messages, profile text and photos are checked automatically, including by our AI model provider. Messages that fail are not delivered. Explicit photos are deleted, and photos can also be refused for other reasons, such as showing contact details or no face. Automated checks can make mistakes and can miss content. Serious violations, or statements suggesting that a user is under 18, can place an account on hold automatically and send it to a moderator. Only a person can ban an account.

Conversation coaching

Suki reads your messages with a match to offer coaching to you both. This is on by default, and you can turn it off with "Let Suki read my chats" in Settings. Your words are then no longer used for coaching either of you. Safety checks still apply to all messages, including automated review of recent messages in a conversation that has been flagged.

Who can see your information

  • People you're introduced to and match with see your public profile and photos, and matches see the messages you send them. Your exact birthdate, email address and dealbreakers aren't shown to them, and neither is what you tell Suki privately.
  • Our moderators and administrators can review reports, the content included in them, and records of automated safety decisions, and can access other data where needed to run or secure the Service.
  • Service providers, listed below, process data for us.
  • Authorities and others, where the law requires it or where we need to protect someone from serious harm. We report suspected child exploitation.
  • A successor, if the Service is transferred as part of a reorganization or sale. We would tell you first.

International transfers

We store data in the United States and send the data shown above to China for AI processing. These countries may not have the same data protection laws as where you live, and authorities there may be able to request access to data under local law. By using the Service, you understand that your data is processed in these countries. Where the law requires it, we will put appropriate safeguards in place for these transfers.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • edit your profile, or ask Suki to correct anything on it, including your birthdate;
  • read and delete Suki's notes about you, and reset what she has learned;
  • turn off coaching and post-date check-ins, and leave her voice off;
  • block and report people, and see and end your signed-in sessions;
  • export your account details, profile, photo records, settings, conversation with Suki, messages you sent and her notes. The export doesn't include image files or internal evaluations;
  • delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be subject to certain automated decisions. To use these rights, write to support@heros-agent.space from your account's email address, or tell us the name on your account if you signed in with WeChat. We may need to confirm that it's you, and we won't treat you differently for asking. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Connections to the Service use HTTPS. Passwords, for accounts that have one, are stored as Argon2id hashes, session tokens are stored as hashes, and photos are kept in a private storage bucket with encryption at rest. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is completely secure, so if you use a password, please make it unique. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

SynAgent is only for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor is using the Service, write to us, and we'll hold the account and delete its data as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We'll change the date at the top when we update this policy. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
