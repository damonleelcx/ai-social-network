Child safety standards

SynAgent, including the Suki app, is operated by Heros Agents and is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind.

What is prohibited

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including drawn, edited or AI-generated images;
  • sexualizing a minor, or asking for or offering sexual content involving a minor;
  • grooming, sextortion, or arranging to meet a minor for sexual purposes;
  • trafficking or any other exploitation of a minor;
  • using the Service if you are under 18.

How we prevent it

  • Everyone gives a birthdate during setup. A birthdate under 18 stops setup and holds the account for review.
  • Messages and profile text are checked automatically. Sexual content involving a minor is not delivered, and the account is held at once.
  • Photos are checked automatically before other people can see them. Sexual images of a minor are deleted and reported to our moderators, and a photo that may show someone under 18 waits for a person to review it.
  • If someone tells Suki they are under 18, Suki stops helping with dating and the account is held for review.

How to report

  • In the app, use the report option on a person or in a chat.
  • By email, write to support@heros-agent.space, our point of contact for child safety. Please do not attach images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services first.

What we do

A person reviews every report of this kind as a priority. When we find a violation, we remove the content and permanently ban the account.

We report apparent CSAM and child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or to the relevant authority, and we comply with child safety laws wherever we operate.
