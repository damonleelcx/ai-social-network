Protection of minors

SynAgent is operated by Nanjing Zhiyu Wujie Technology Co., Ltd. and is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not allow any form of harm to minors.

Prohibited conduct

  • Any sexual content involving minors, including drawn, edited or AI-generated images;
  • Asking for or providing inappropriate content involving minors;
  • Luring or coercing minors, or arranging to meet minors for improper purposes;
  • Any other harm to minors;
  • Using the Service while under 18.

How we prevent it

  • Everyone must give their birthday during setup. If the birthday shows the person is under 18, setup stops and the account is suspended pending review.
  • Messages and profile text go through automated checks. Inappropriate content involving minors is not sent, and the account is suspended immediately.
  • Photos go through automated checks before others can see them. Photos that may involve minors wait for review by a person.
  • If someone tells Suki they are under 18, Suki stops helping them find companions, and the account is suspended pending review.

How to report

  • In the Mini Program, use the report feature on a profile or in a chat.
  • Write to support@zhiyuwujie.top. Please do not attach images.
  • If a minor is in immediate danger, call 110 (police) right away.

What we do

Every such report is handled with priority by a person. If a violation is found, we delete the content, permanently ban the account, and report to the public security authorities and other relevant authorities as required by law.
