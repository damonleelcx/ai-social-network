Privacy Policy

Last revised on 7 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Nanjing Zhiyu Wujie Technology Co., Ltd. ("we") collects through the SynAgent WeChat Mini Program and this website (suki.zhiyuwujie.top, together the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have. If you have questions or requests, please email support@zhiyuwujie.top.

Summary

  • SynAgent helps you find company for doing things together, such as watching a movie, sharing a meal, taking a trip, working out or studying.
  • For accounts registered through the Service, personal information is stored on servers within the People's Republic of China.
  • We do not sell your personal information, do not show ads, and do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can view, download a copy of and delete your information, or close your account, at any time under "Me".

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: accounts, conversations with Suki, introducing companions, chat, arranging meetups, and voice playback.
  • To improve introductions: learning what kind of people you like to do things with.
  • To keep people safe: checking content, handling reports, and preventing abuse and fraud.
  • To keep things running: troubleshooting, securing our systems, and backing up data.
  • To meet legal obligations: cooperating with requests from the relevant authorities as required by law.

We do not use your information to train our own AI models.

Automated decision-making

Suki automatically makes introductions between people in the same city. The criteria you set first filter the people considered, who are then ranked based on your profile, the introductions you accepted and skipped, your feedback after meetups, and an AI model's judgment of whether you two would get along. We do not score photos for looks. You can write to support@zhiyuwujie.top to ask us to explain an automated decision or to have a person review it.

Content moderation

The Service does not allow pornographic, violent or other illegal content. Messages, profile text and photos go through automated checks, including checks performed by our AI model provider. Messages that fail the checks are not sent, and photos that break the rules are deleted. Content that seriously breaks the rules or shows that a user is under 18 causes the account to be suspended and passed to moderators. Only staff can ban an account.

Who can see your information

  • People who are introduced to you or team up with you can see your public profile and photos, and the messages you send them. They cannot see your exact birthday, your email, or what you tell Suki privately.
  • Our moderators and administrators can view relevant information when handling reports or keeping the Service running.
  • The service providers listed below process relevant information when providing services to us.
  • The relevant authorities, when required by laws and regulations, or to protect others from serious harm.

Your rights

Within the Service you can:

  • View and edit your profile, or ask Suki to correct it;
  • View and delete Suki's notes about you, and reset what she has learned;
  • Turn off chat suggestions and after-meetup check-ins, and turn off voice;
  • Block and report others, and view and end sign-in sessions;
  • Download a copy of your account information, profile, settings, conversations with Suki and the messages you have sent;
  • Close your account.

You may also, as provided by law, access, copy, correct, supplement and delete your personal information, withdraw your consent, and ask us to explain our rules for processing personal information. Please write from your account email to support@zhiyuwujie.top; if you sign in with WeChat, please tell us your account nickname. We will reply within 15 working days after verifying your identity.

Security

The Service uses HTTPS encryption for all traffic. Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes, sign-in credentials are stored as hashes, and access to production systems is strictly limited. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will inform you and report it to the relevant authorities as required by law.

Minors

The Service is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you find that a minor is using the Service, please contact us, and we will suspend the account and delete the related information as required by law.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we revise the "Last revised" line at the top of the page. We will tell you about significant changes through the Service or by email before they take effect.
